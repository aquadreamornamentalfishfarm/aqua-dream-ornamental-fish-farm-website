Welcome to Aqua Dream Ornamental Fish Farm

Aqua Dream Ornamental Fish Farm is an ornamental fish farm based in Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, India.

We focus on providing healthy and attractive ornamental fish for aquarium hobbyists, breeders and fish lovers.

Our current collection includes different varieties of guppies, bettas and live food cultures for aquarium fish.

We aim to make quality ornamental fish accessible to hobbyists while providing friendly support before and after purchase.

Our Story

Aqua Dream started with a passion for ornamental fish keeping and aquarium culture.

What began as an interest in keeping and breeding beautiful aquarium fish has grown into Aqua Dream Ornamental Fish Farm.

We continue to expand our collection of ornamental fish varieties while improving our breeding, maintenance and fish-care practices.

Our goal is to build a trusted connection between our farm and aquarium hobbyists who love keeping and breeding ornamental fish.
